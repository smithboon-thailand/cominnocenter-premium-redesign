# Premium Design Guidelines
## ComInnoCenter – World-Class Redesign Direction

**Goal**: Transform the current clean but basic Wix site into a **super premium**, academic-yet-cutting-edge digital presence that positions the Center as Thailand’s (and ASEAN’s) leading authority in Communication Innovation for Quality of Life & Sustainability.

---

### 1. Brand Positioning & Tone
- **Essence**: Intelligent · Human-centered · Innovative · Trustworthy · Impactful
- **Feeling**: Like a top-tier research institute + creative studio hybrid (think: IDEO × Harvard Kennedy × high-end Thai academic elegance)
- **Audience**: Government agencies, international organizations (ILO, WHO, ASEAN), corporate CSR/ESG teams, universities, foundations, policy makers, high-potential students & researchers.

### 2. Visual Identity Direction (Premium Evolution)

**Official Color Foundation** (ต้องเคารพอย่างเคร่งครัด):
- **สีชมพู (Pink)** = สีประจำมหาวิทยาลัยจุฬาลงกรณ์มหาวิทยาลัย
- **สีน้ำเงิน (Blue)** = สีประจำคณะนิเทศศาสตร์
- **สีหลากหลาย (Multicolor)** = สีจาก SDG Goals ของ UN ที่สื่อถึง Sustainability

**Premium Color System Recommendation**:

| Role | Color | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Primary Brand** | Chulalongkorn Pink (refined) | Logo, key CTAs, hero accents, important highlights |
| **Secondary Brand** | Faculty Blue (deep, sophisticated) | Navigation, headings, secondary buttons, trust elements |
| **Neutral Base** | Warm off-white / soft bone + deep charcoal | Backgrounds, body text, large surfaces |
| **Sustainability Accents** | Selected SDG colors (used elegantly & sparingly) | Icons, data visualization, impact metrics, sustainability-related sections, progress indicators |
| **Support** | Soft gradients between pink–blue, subtle metallic/gold for prestige | Dividers, hover states, special moments |

**How to use SDG Multicolors Premium-style**:
- ห้ามใช้สีรุ้งแบบเด็กๆ หรือ rainbow เต็มหน้า
- ใช้เฉพาะสีที่เกี่ยวข้องกับเป้าหมายจริง (เช่น Goal 3 Health, Goal 4 Education, Goal 11 Cities, Goal 13 Climate ฯลฯ)
- ใช้เป็น accent เล็กๆ, icon set, หรือ chart ที่สวยและมีเจตนา
- สามารถสร้าง “SDG palette” ที่ถูกปรับความอิ่มตัว (desaturated slightly) ให้ดูหรูและเป็นผู้ใหญ่

**Typography**:
- Headings: High-end geometric or neo-grotesque with character (e.g. Neue Haas Grotesk, Suisse Int’l, or custom variable font). Thai + English dual support must be excellent.
- Body: Highly readable, elegant serif or humanist sans for long academic text.
- Large, confident display type for hero statements.

**Imagery & Motion**:
- Cinematic photography: real impact moments, thoughtful portraits of leadership/team, behind-the-scenes of workshops & fieldwork, abstract visualizations of communication flows / AR / data.
- Subtle, high-quality micro-interactions and scroll-triggered animations (not gimmicky).
- Custom motion graphics for services (especially AR, Video, Campaign).
- 3D or generative abstract forms that feel intelligent and organic. Can incorporate refined geometric shapes inspired by the current pink starburst but elevated.

**Logo Evolution**:
- Keep recognition of current colorful logo (which already combines university pink + multicolor SDG energy) for transition period.
- Create a refined primary wordmark + monogram for premium contexts that can work in pure pink, pure blue, or elegant multicolor versions.

### 3. UX / Information Architecture (Premium Experience)

**Structure Proposal**:
1. **Home** – Cinematic hero + impact statement + key services preview + selected case studies + team highlight + CTA to collaborate.
2. **About / The Center** – Story, Mission/Vision/Objectives, Leadership deep profiles, full team, networks & partners.
3. **Expertise / Services** – Deep, elegant pages for each service area with process, outcomes, related works.
4. **Impact / Works** – Filterable case studies / projects with rich storytelling (challenge → approach → outcome → impact metrics if available).
5. **Knowledge / Insights** – Publications, e-books, research outputs, media mentions.
6. **Collaborate / Contact** – High-touch inquiry form, partnership opportunities, booking for training/seminar.

**Experience Principles**:
- Generous white space (or off-white space) with intentional density.
- Smooth, purposeful scrolling narratives.
- Fast performance (Next.js / Astro or similar modern stack recommended).
- Fully bilingual (Thai primary + English) with elegant language switcher.
- Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA+) as non-negotiable for public-impact organization.
- Mobile-first but desktop feels luxurious and expansive.

### 4. Key Premium Features to Include
- Interactive service explorer or visual process diagrams.
- Case study immersive pages with video embeds, before/after, testimonial quotes.
- Dynamic team grid with hover bio expansions.
- Research / publication searchable library.
- Subtle particle or generative background elements that respond to scroll (very restrained).
- High-quality video backgrounds or short loops on key sections.
- Partnership / client logo wall with elegant hover states.
- Newsletter / knowledge update subscription with premium feel.
- SDG impact visualization that feels sophisticated (not cartoonish).

### 5. Technical & Implementation Recommendations
- Modern stack: Next.js 15 + Tailwind + Framer Motion (or GSAP) + Sanity / Payload CMS for content.
- Hosting: Vercel or high-performance edge.
- Image: Next Image + Cloudinary / Imgix for optimization.
- Analytics + privacy-first.
- SEO optimized for both Thai and English academic/professional search terms.

### 6. Moodboard Keywords
Quiet luxury academic · Intelligent creativity · Human impact · Sustainable future · Thai excellence with global standard · Clean power · Soft precision · Warm intellect · Official pink & blue with thoughtful SDG energy.
